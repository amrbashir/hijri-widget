Requires **Android 8.0 or later**. Works offline, with no ads or account required.

## Features

- **Make each widget your own.** Customize placed widgets independently and preview changes
  against your wallpaper before saving.
- **Match your home screen.** Choose Material You colors on Android 12+, custom colors, or a
  transparent background. Adjust the font, size, weight, shadow, alignment, and padding.
- **Arrange your date.** Start with a preset, move components between rows in the visual
  editor, or enter a custom date pattern.
- **Use your language.** Follow the device language or choose a language for each date
  component, including mixed-language formats. The app interface supports English and Arabic.
- **Follow your calendar.** Choose Islamic, Islamic Umm al-Qura, or Islamic Civil calculation,
  adjust the Hijri date by up to ±2 days, and set the time when a new Hijri day begins.
